What Happens Next

After You Submit Your Inquiry

I review every project inquiry personally, usually within 48 hours.

From there, I'll follow up with a proposal outlining scope, deliverables, and timeline, shaped by the type of engagement, format, and how urgent your timeline is.

Once confirmed, the real work starts. Depending on the engagement, that might mean a discovery session to map the actual problem, an audit of what's already been built, or the first working sync with your team.

For ongoing engagements, I set a clear cadence upfront, regular check-ins and defined deliverables, so nothing gets built in a vacuum.

I take on a limited number of engagements at a time so that each one gets my full attention. If your timeline is tight, mention that in the project details above and I'll prioritize your inquiry.

Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered

Do you work with early-stage startups, or only established organizations?

Both. The size of the organization matters less to me than whether the problem is real and someone is actually ready to act on it.

Are engagements remote?

Yes, remote-first. I work with teams and founders across Nigeria and internationally, and can travel for the right engagement.

What if I'm not sure which category my project fits?

That's normal, many of the most interesting problems don't fit neatly into product, governance, or design on their own. Reach out and describe the problem; we'll figure out the shape of the work together.

Do you take on speaking or teaching engagements?

Yes. I've trained professionals from CEOs to interns since 2021, and I'm open to workshops, guest lectures, and keynotes on product, AI governance, and design.
