Research interests

Evaluation capacity in countries that import AI

Most AI systems used in Nigeria are built elsewhere. I am working on what a country needs in order to test those systems itself, before and after deployment, instead of relying on the developer's own claims.

Under way.

Adversarial attacks on RAG systems

I built a hybrid retrieval pipeline (FAISS, TF-IDF, cross-encoder reranking, FLAN-T5), attacked it with synonym substitution and word deletion, and scored the damage with an Adversarial Risk Index I developed.

Completed as my undergraduate thesis.
